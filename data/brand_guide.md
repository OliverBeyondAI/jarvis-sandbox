# Acme Corp — Brand Voice & Style Guide

## Brand Overview

**Company:** Acme Corp
**Tagline:** "Building the future, one innovation at a time."
**Industry:** Technology & Innovation

## Voice & Tone

### Core Voice Attributes
- **Confident** — We speak with authority, backed by expertise and evidence.
- **Approachable** — We avoid jargon. We explain complex topics simply.
- **Forward-looking** — We focus on what's next, not what was.
- **Human** — We're a team of people, not a faceless corporation.

### Tone by Channel
| Channel        | Tone                                      |
|----------------|-------------------------------------------|
| LinkedIn       | Professional, thought-leadership, concise |
| Twitter/X      | Punchy, conversational, witty             |
| Blog           | In-depth, educational, narrative          |
| Instagram      | Visual-first, inspiring, casual           |

## Writing Guidelines

### Do
- Use active voice ("We launched..." not "It was launched...")
- Lead with the insight, not the setup
- Include a clear call-to-action
- Use data and specifics over vague claims
- Keep sentences under 25 words where possible

### Don't
- Use buzzwords without substance (e.g., "synergy", "disrupt")
- Over-use exclamation marks (max one per post)
- Make claims we can't back up
- Use all-caps for emphasis
- Post without a clear audience in mind

## Post Structure Templates

### LinkedIn (Thought Leadership)
```
[Hook — bold claim or surprising stat]

[2-3 sentences expanding on the insight]

[What this means for our audience]

[CTA: question, link, or invitation to engage]

[Hashtags]
```

### Twitter/X (Engagement)
```
[Punchy hook — max 1 line]

[Key insight in 1-2 sentences]

[CTA or question]

[Hashtags]
```

## Brand Colors (for reference in visual posts)

## Emoji Usage
- Use sparingly (max 2-3 per post)
- Preferred: rocket, lightning, chart-up, lightbulb, globe
- Avoid: fire, 100, prayer hands (overused)
